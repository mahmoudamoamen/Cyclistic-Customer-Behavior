# Cyclistic-Customer-Behavior

##  Project Overview
In a bike-share company in Chicago. The director of marketing believes the company’s future success depends on maximizing the number of annual memberships, and he wants me to help him

---

##  Business Task & Objectives
The primary objective is to analyze historical trip data to uncover distinct usage patterns between **casual riders** (single-pass or daily-pass users) and **annual members**.

### Key Questions:
1. How do annual members and casual riders use bikes differently?
2. How do ride durations vary across user types?
3. What are the usage trends throughout the days of the week?
4. What are the user preferences regarding bike types?

## Analytical Approach & Metrics

The analysis evaluated user behavior across five main areas:

1. **Overall Ride Duration:** Compared the average time taken per trip by annual members vs. casual riders.
2. **User Volume:** Compared the total number of annual members vs. casual riders.
3. **Weekly Duration Trends:** Analyzed average time consumed by both user types for each day of the week.
4. **Weekly Volume Trends:** Tracked rider volume patterns across weekdays vs. weekends/holidays.
5. **Bike Type Utilization:** Identified the most popular bike types for each member group and average duration spent on each type.

---

## Key Findings & Summary

| Metric | Annual Members | Casual Riders |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Ride Duration** | Shorter periods | Longer periods |
| **Usage Pattern** | Regular & consistent | Irregular & sporadic |
| **Weekly Peak** | Steady throughout the week | Spikes on weekends & holidays |
| **Bike Type Preference** | Avoid certain types (e.g., docked bikes) | Use all bike types (including docked bikes) |

### Summary Points:
1. **Regularity:** Annual members use their bikes regularly, but for shorter periods of time per ride.
2. **Duration:** Casual members use their bikes irregularly, but for longer periods of time per ride.
3. **Schedule:** Casual members use their bikes on holidays and weekends significantly more than during the rest of the week.
4. **Bike Selection:** There are specific bike types (such as docked bikes) that annual members do not use.

---

## Conclusion

* **Annual Members** use their bikes primarily for regular daily tasks, such as commuting to work or school.
* **Casual Members** mostly use their bikes for entertainment, recreation, and leisure.

---

## Strategic Recommendations

1. **Targeted Leisure Conversions:** Launch weekend-focused promotions targeting casual riders during holidays to show the value of an annual pass.
2. **Flexible/Seasonal Memberships:** Offer weekend-only or seasonal plans tailored to recreational users.
3. **Commute Incentive Campaigns:** Market the efficiency of bike commutes to casual riders during leisure rides.
